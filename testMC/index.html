Our Credo

Bound by an Ironclad Code

We are a law-abiding international three-piece motorcycle club — a global, united front with Clans scattered across the world, all bound by the same ironclad code observed by every Member. We stand shoulder-to-shoulder with the Alliance of Law Abiding clubs.

⚔️

Loyalty & Honor

A brotherhood built on loyalty, honor, and the roar of the open highway. Respect is a core principle that extends to everyone.

🛡️

Law-Abiding

We exist within the law and uphold it. We believe in the rights guaranteed by the constitution and the freedom to assemble and forge our own path.

🔥

Forged in Fire

We welcome active military, veterans, everyday heroes from law enforcement and EMS, and riders from all walks of life in between.

Who We Are

More Than a Motorcycle Club

We exist because we believe in the ironclad rights guaranteed by the constitution and the freedom to assemble and forge our own path without the permission of any other entity.

In the Iron Vikings MC, respect is a core principle that extends to everyone. Fear? We leave that for the weak.

The IVMC is more than a motorcycle club — it is a brotherhood built on loyalty, honor, and the roar of the open highway.

Charity & Community

We Ride for More Than Ourselves

Roughly 80% of our time and resources go to charitable, educational, and community support activities — benefit rides for families in crisis, motorcycle safety training, food drives, emergency assistance, and wellness checks, year-round.

🏍️

Benefit Rides

Major fundraising and ride events scheduled quarterly, supporting families in crisis and local causes.

🎓

Safety & Education

Workshops on motorcycle laws and rider safety, held at least twice annually — in person and online.

🤝

Community Support

Food drives, emergency assistance, and wellness checks for members, families, and neighbors.
